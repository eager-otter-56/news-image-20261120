A one-image summary of the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026**, drawn as a newspaper front page:

- **Three Powers. One Table.** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first-ever trilateral summit in Shenzhen and sign a joint peace declaration.
- **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the UN's top court in The Hague rules the campaign against the Rohingya Muslims was genocide.
- **Cyclone Akvan slams India** — landfall at Gopalpur, Odisha; 300,000 evacuated, winds to 90 km/h.
- **Fossil fuels erased at COP31** — the draft climate deal drops the fossil-fuel phase-out.
- **Crimea Bridge hit again** — Ukraine strikes once more; Putin refuses a truce.

Entry for the make.fun competition [news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## Run

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to the output directory — `/output` by default. No network, deterministic output.
